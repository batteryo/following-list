    Following them

    Press Follow to open that account on X with X's Follow prompt, or click anywhere else on the row to just open the profile. Both open in a new tab, and a middle-click opens them in the background, so you can line up a batch. Opened accounts get a check mark, and this browser remembers them.

    Where the list comes from

    The page asks FxTwitter, a free public service that reads X, for the account and its list, and loads profile pictures from X. It needs no login. Long lists take a while, roughly a minute for every 3,000 accounts.
